Salt Flats

The salt flats is a desert of white salt that spans 30-32,000 acres. Its located northwestern Utah west of the Great Salt Lake along Interstate 80. You should come to experience one of the most unique natural landscape.

Reasons

  1. The vast, blindingly white salt crust creates an alien-like expanse. During the winter or after a spring rain, a thin layer of water turns the flats into a giant natural mirror reflecting the sky and clouds.
  2. The flats are an international hub for motorsports and land-speed trials. You can visit the Bonneville International Speedway to watch high-speed events like Speed Week in August or see where historic land-speed records were set.
